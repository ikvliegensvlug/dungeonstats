# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Bosses: which bosses of the dungeon or raid you killed, which beat you and which are still to go (Bosses 3/8), each with its face. A face is learned the first time you target a boss, mouse over it or see its nameplate. Kills are seen from the end of the boss fight, the "dies, you gain experience" line, the game's kill events, a looted corpse or a dead boss as your target. New WoW Forever dungeons show the bosses you fought.
- Players who left the group keep their face on the scoreboard.
- Hover a card to see the top 5 of that category (with DPS for damage).
- Click a player's card or loot row to whisper them, for example to ask for a trade; shift-click a loot row to link the item.
- Loot in two columns with pages of 10, with who got each item and whether they won it with Need or Greed.
- Fixed: rolls and winners were not read on WoW Forever, which puts a [Loot] link in front of these lines.
- Post to party shares blue and purple items only.
- Keeps your last 100 runs instead of 5.

## 1.0.0

- First version, split off from ChatBanner: a scoreboard when you leave a dungeon or raid with most damage (with DPS), most healing, most damage taken and who died most, each with the player's portrait, from the game's own damage meter.
- Loot with who got it, the winning roll and who else needed; best items first, with a quality filter (green, blue or purple and better).
- Post to party or raid, Older and Newer for the last 5 runs, `/dstats` for the run so far.
- Takes over the runs and loot setting saved by ChatBanner.
